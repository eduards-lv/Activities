This is the database of students with their corresponding lectures. For each student has related lectures with relation to enrollment.

Each lecture is related to lecturer and to subject.

Additional attributes: Student may also have student card number, enrollment may also have end date, lectures may also have length, lecturers may also have phone, subjects may also have description.

Relationships: Enrollments to students: one to many, Students to lectures: many to many, lecturers to lectures many to many (or one to many if two lecturers can't teach during one lecture), lectures to subjects: one to many.')
